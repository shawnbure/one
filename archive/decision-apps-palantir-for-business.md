## What qualifies as a decision app

1. **Recurring ritual** — done monthly/quarterly, forever.
2. **Multi-department, multi-person** — several people pull from several systems by hand.
3. **Compile-then-decide** — most effort is *assembling* the data; the decision waits on it.
4. **Blended data only the catalog can join** — the value comes from *combining* silos, not any one.
5. **Stale on arrival** — by the time the report's built, it's out of date. The app is always-live.

## The flagship menu

### ⭐ 1. Business Review — the always-live exec/board report
- **Ritual it kills:** the monthly/quarterly fire drill where finance, sales, ops, and every dept head spend days assembling a board deck that's stale the moment it's printed.
- **Today:** 5–10 people, several days, every single month, forever.
- **Blends:** revenue, pipeline, cash, headcount, support load, delivery/project status — every department's slice.
- **Open app → decide:** run the business meeting off a live, drill-downable model. *Most universal ritual — every company does this.*

### ⭐ 2. True Profitability — real margin by customer / product / project
- **Ritual it kills:** the "which customers/products/projects actually make money" analysis nobody trusts, done ad hoc in spreadsheets — if at all.
- **Today:** finance + ops + delivery reconcile revenue vs. cost vs. labor by hand, rarely, out of date.
- **Blends:** invoices/revenue + vendor/COGS + labor/time (payroll, timesheets) + delivery effort (projects, tickets).
- **Open app → decide:** fire unprofitable accounts, reprice, refocus. *Most companies genuinely do not know this.*

### ⭐ 3. Account Intelligence — health, churn & growth across the whole book
- **Ritual it kills:** the quarterly at-risk / QBR / expansion review, compiled from five systems by hand.
- **Today:** CS + sales + support + finance assemble health scorecards manually.
- **Blends:** pipeline/revenue + support tickets/sentiment + payment history + product usage + comms recency.
- **Open app → decide:** who to save, grow, or drop — continuously, not quarterly.

### 4. Quote/Order-to-Cash — lifecycle flow & where revenue is stuck
- **Ritual it kills:** the cross-dept email chains — "where's this order / why isn't this paid / what's stuck?"
- **Today:** sales-ops, fulfillment, and finance each see one slice; nobody sees end-to-end.
- **Blends:** CRM deal → contract → order/fulfillment → invoice → payment.
- **Open app → decide:** unstick revenue, cut cycle time, find leakage.

### 5. Spend & Vendor Intelligence — what we actually pay, to whom, and our exposure
- **Ritual it kills:** the annual procurement/spend audit; the hunt for duplicate and maverick spend.
- **Today:** finance + each department reconcile payments, contracts, and budgets by hand.
- **Blends:** AP/payments + contracts/obligations + department budgets + vendor records.
- **Open app → decide:** consolidate vendors, cut duplicate spend, manage renewal exposure.

### 6. Capacity & Utilization — demand vs. supply (services/ops-heavy orgs)
- **Ritual it kills:** the staffing/utilization spreadsheet reconciling pipeline against people.
- **Today:** sales pipeline + delivery/projects + HR/staffing + time tracking, reconciled manually.
- **Blends:** pipeline (CRM) + project allocation + headcount/skills + time.
- **Open app → decide:** hire, bench, prioritize, forecast delivery.

### 7. Forecast Reality — a forecast grounded in evidence, not rep optimism
- **Ritual it kills:** the sandbagged spreadsheet forecast nobody believes.
- **Today:** sales-ops builds from rep judgment; finance adjusts by gut.
- **Blends:** CRM pipeline + historical conversion patterns + delivery capacity + seasonality.
- **Open app → decide:** commit to a number backed by the company's own history.

*(Further candidates: Audit/Compliance Evidence Room · Incident/Escalation Timeline · Knowledge Continuity when a key person leaves.)*

## Honest caveats (these are hard — which is why they're the moat)

- **They need the structured plane mature** — entity resolution + accurate cross-system blending. Not hour-2 apps; they're the *gets-indispensable-over-weeks* tier.
- **The data must exist and be connectable.** True Profitability needs cost/time data — if a client doesn't track time or costs cleanly, no app can conjure it. Assess data availability per client. *(This is exactly where the services / light forward-deployed model earns its keep — like Palantir's FDEs, but productized.)*
- **Accuracy and trust matter more here** — these drive big decisions. Provenance, confidence, and human validation are non-negotiable; a wrong profitability number burns trust instantly.
- **Bigger build, more strategic buyer** — but the ROI (analyst-weeks eliminated + better decisions) is enormous, so the deal is bigger too.
