workrr one — How It's Built & Deployed

A private business-intelligence platform that runs entirely inside your own Cloudflare account.

workrr one connects your business systems into a private, entity-resolved, time-aware model of your business (the “catalog”), and serves decision apps on top of it. It is built on Cloudflare's developer platform and deployed 100% within your own Cloudflare account — no workrr-hosted servers, no third-party model calls, no data egress.

Deployment model — your account, always

Every workrr one component is provisioned inside your Cloudflare account. workrr deploys and operates it there under scoped, auditable, revocable access — like a forward-deployed team with keys to one room, not a vendor holding your data.

  • Compute, storage, vectors, and inference all live in your tenant.
  • Your data never transits workrr infrastructure and is never sent to a model vendor.
  • Nothing is shared across customers; there is no multi-tenant pool.

Privacy & security

  • In-account: all data and compute stay in your Cloudflare tenant.
  • No model-vendor egress: inference runs on Workers AI (or your AI Gateway); prompts and data are not sent to OpenAI/Anthropic/etc.
  • No training on your data, ever; no cross-customer pooling.
  • Zero Trust identity for every user; workrr operator access is scoped, logged, and revocable.
  • Data localization controls keep data in-region where required.
  • Sourced answers: every result traces to the record it came from — auditable, not a black box.

Why this beats plain RAG or BI

The catalog has two planes

Semantic + structured, together.

  • Semantic: search & ask across all content.
  • Structured: entity-resolved, time-aware facts with provenance.

What that enables

  • Joins the same customer/deal/dollar across systems.
  • Answers “what was true when,” not just “what's a match.”
  • Drives decisions, not just document snippets or static charts.

What a pilot looks like

  • Weeks, not quarters. Connect 2–3 systems, stand up one decision app, validate on real data.
  • Scoped & low-risk: runs in your account; remove access and it's gone.
  • Serverless economics: scales with usage, no idle infrastructure, R2 with no egress fees.
