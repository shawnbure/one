# HHS Research Integrity successor-cycle plan

## Decision

Do not submit a rushed FY2026 application. The July 27, 2026 notice requires an active SAM.gov and Grants.gov registration, complete SF-424/SF-424A/SF-LLL forms, project abstract, double-spaced project narrative, appendices, detailed federal budget, measurable empirical outcomes, partner commitments, research environment, and a defensible human-subjects/IRB approach. It anticipates only two awards.

Workrr should prepare a consortium for a successor notice or a related research-integrity solicitation.

## Concept

**Working title:** Does Evidence-Governed AI Reduce Research-Record and Authorship Integrity Failures?

**Focus area:** Examine how artificial intelligence acts as a driver of research integrity or a risk factor for research misconduct.

**Primary question:** Do release-pinned AI configurations, provenance-linked execution records, disclosure prompts, human approval, and immutable evidence reduce undisclosed AI authorship, unsupported claims, record-keeping gaps, and irreproducible transformations compared with ordinary generative-AI use?

## Proposed design

Use a qualified university or research institution as the empirical-research lead and IRB/FWA home. Workrr supplies the instrumented research workflow and engineering team.

Candidate study:

- 120-240 consenting researchers or research staff across at least two institutions.
- Randomized or stepped-wedge comparison of ordinary approved AI tools versus evidence-governed use.
- Standardized tasks covering literature synthesis, data-transformation planning, record annotation, drafting, and decision support.
- Pre-registered primary outcomes: unsupported factual claims, undisclosed AI contributions, provenance completeness, reproducibility of transformations, policy compliance, and time burden.
- Secondary outcomes: user trust, perceived administrative burden, correction latency, false-positive governance alerts, and usability.
- Independent blinded adjudication where feasible.
- Public protocol, de-identified aggregate findings, implementation guide, and freely available policy/training materials.

## Required partners

1. Research-integrity office or RIO with 42 C.F.R. Part 93 expertise.
2. Empirical PI with study-design and statistical-analysis experience.
3. Institution with an FWA and IRB capable of reviewing the study.
4. At least one additional research institution for external validity.
5. Responsible-conduct-of-research training specialist.
6. Workrr as technology and instrumentation partner.
