## Subcontract concept

**Name:** Governed AI Supplier Operations Lab

**Purpose:** Help small suppliers in a selected strategic Arizona industry improve the operational workflows that block qualification, production readiness, quality documentation, exception handling, and buyer responsiveness.

**Candidate industry:** Select exactly one based on the prime's network:

- semiconductor and advanced-manufacturing suppliers;
- aerospace and defense suppliers;
- energy/critical-infrastructure suppliers;
- health-manufacturing suppliers.

**Workrr scope:**

1. Baseline one high-friction supplier workflow per participant.
2. Identify defects, delays, missing evidence, and system handoff failures.
3. Deploy a governed AI-assisted workflow using the participant's existing systems.
4. Retain human approval and customer-controlled data boundaries.
5. Measure cycle time, rework, exception backlog, on-time response, and audit completeness.
6. Produce a reusable supplier-readiness playbook and anonymized aggregate findings.

**Proposed cohort:** 10-20 small suppliers selected by the prime and industry partners.

**Proposed Workrr subcontract:** $100,000-$180,000, subject to the final prime budget and procurement rules.

## Outcomes

- At least 80% of participating suppliers complete workflow baselines.
- At least 10 suppliers deploy one production workflow.
- Median target workflow cycle time improves by at least 25%.
- Documentation or exception rework falls by at least 20%.
- Each participating supplier leaves with a control/evidence map and commercialization plan.
- Prime produces industry-level lessons without disclosing supplier confidential information.

These are proposal targets, not claims of achieved performance.
